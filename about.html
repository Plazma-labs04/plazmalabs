Our Story

Serving, Protecting, Enriching Lives

Plazma Labs is a premier pharmaceutical company based in Mumbai, with a rich 22-year legacy of excellence in healthcare. Dedicated to delivering high-quality and affordable medicines, we manufacture our products under strict compliance with WHO-GMP, ISO, and Halal certifications.

We offer a diverse portfolio of advanced formulations to meet patient needs across various therapeutic segments — blending science and care to ensure the health and well-being of your loved ones.

Dosage FormsTablets, Capsules, Syrups, Dry Syrups, Injections and Powders.
Therapeutic RangeHigh-end oral antibiotics, anti-ulcerants, anti-inflammatory pain relievers, cough syrups and Ayurvedic medicines.
Nutritional SupplementsPremium Iron, Calcium and Protein formulations.
